The Challenge

Moving beyond engineering scale.

Infrastructure is difficult to communicate. People don't experience ports, transmission lines, or renewable energy projects directly—they experience what those assets make possible.

In the wake of a period of intense public and market scrutiny, the Adani Group unveiled a multi-media campaign titled ‘Hum Karke Dikhate Hain’. Developed by Ogilvy India, the 100-second television commercial celebrated 35 years of service, emphasizing resilience and converting challenges into opportunities.

However, the primary strategic challenge was not merely awareness, but coherence. The Group operates independently governed, market-leading businesses across energy, logistics, materials, and consumer sectors. The opportunity was to transform a singular corporate statement into a believable, unified public narrative that felt consistent at a group level while remaining authentic to each business unit.

My Role

Converting TVC into an ongoing system.

Working within the Corporate Brand Custodian team, my role was to convert a television campaign into an ongoing communication system across paid, earned, and owned media.

Cross-Business Alignment Reviewing and aligning communication across 10+ business units and agency partners to ensure unified messaging.

Multilingual Rollout Supporting localized execution across 10+ languages and 28 regional markets.
The Strategy

The two-phase narrative evolution.

The strategic framework moved through two distinct chapters to shift perception from stats to felt progress.

Phase One: Scale & Resilience

Established the Group's identity around resilience, execution, and nation-building. The central thought was simple: "We don't just make promises. We deliver."

Phase Two: Human Storytelling

Evolved from corporate statements to human impact. Instead of showcasing infrastructure, we showcased the lives transformed because that infrastructure existed.

"Infrastructure became the backdrop. People became the protagonists."

Relatable protagonists and memorable lines reframed the industrial giant into an enabler of everyday progress:

Pehle Pankha, Phir Bijli A child's conviction in a toy fan becomes an aspirational story for green energy and rural electrification.
Journey of Dreams Positions ports and logistics as gateways to opportunity for artisans in Kutch selling handcrafted Namda toys globally.
Suraj Bhaiya Hain Naa Showcases rural economic transformation, schools, and hospitals powered by the Khavda solar power plant.
Aapke Safar Ke Humsafar Elevates airport operations with care, connection, and seamless passenger experience.

Solution

Tone adaptation & credibility management.

I translated the core campaign voice into the natural language of each business unit. Infrastructure services emphasized operational reliability and safety, airports focused on passenger experience, and consumer products communicated quality and everyday usefulness. The core idea remained constant, but the expression matched each audience.

Integrated distribution across advertising, media relations, social platforms, and corporate channels ensured audiences encountered the same idea through different interactions rather than repeated ads. Messaging was sequenced across announcements, stories, and service communications to reinforce recognition over time.
