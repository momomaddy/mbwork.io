Objective

To humanise Adani’s infrastructure by making its impact personally meaningful. The campaign aimed to rebuild trust and positive sentiment among the public, shifting perceptions from cold statistical tonnage to felt progress, and positioning the Group as a consistent force for everyday prosperity and nation-building.

Challenge

In the wake of a period of intense public and market scrutiny, the Adani Group unveiled a multi-media campaign titled ‘Hum Karke Dikhate Hain’. Developed with Ogilvy India, these five television commercials celebrated 35 years of service, emphasizing resilience and converting challenges into opportunities.

Challenges included breaking through cluttered media environments, overcoming category barriers of impersonal heavy infrastructure, engaging diverse demographic audiences, and achieving emotional resonance. Creative hurdles involved crafting relatable human stories, while media constraints required broad, multi-channel reach within disciplined timelines.

However, the primary strategic challenge was not merely awareness, but coherence. The Group operates independently governed, market-leading businesses across energy, logistics, materials, and consumer platforms. The mandate was to transform a singular corporate motto into a believable, unified public narrative that felt consistent at a group level while remaining authentic to each business unit.

Role

Working within the Corporate Brand Custodian team, my responsibility was to convert a high-decibel television campaign into an enduring, operational communication system across paid, earned, and owned media:

Social Storytelling Framework Developed the digital architecture to translate high-level corporate claims into audience-first socio-economic social content.
Cross-Business Alignment Reviewed and aligned narrative rollout across 10+ business units and agency partners to ensure messaging integrity.
Credibility Management Ensured operational updates, service announcements, and executive communications consistently reflected the platform’s promise.
Multilingual Rollout Directed localized and vernacular execution across 10+ Indian languages and 28 regional markets.
Strategy

The strategic framework moved through two distinct chapters to deliberately shift public perception from macro statistics to felt individual progress:

Phase One: Scale & Resilience

Established the Group's identity around execution, resilience, and nation-building. The central thought was clear: "We don't just make promises. We deliver."

Phase Two: Human Storytelling

Evolved from corporate statements to grassroots impact. Instead of showcasing infrastructure, we showcased the lives transformed because that infrastructure exists.

“Infrastructure became the backdrop while people became the protagonists.”

Relatable protagonists and memorable cultural lines reframed the industrial footprint into an everyday partner:

Pehle Pankha, Phir Bijli A child's conviction in a toy fan becomes an aspirational story for green energy and rural electrification.
Journey of Dreams Positions ports and logistics as gateways of opportunity for artisans in Kutch selling handcrafted Namda toys globally.
Suraj Bhaiya Hain Naa Showcases rural economic transformation, schools, and healthcare powered by the Khavda renewable energy cluster.
Aapke Safar Ke Humsafar Elevates airport operations through care, human connection, and seamless passenger hospitality.

Ecosystem

I translated the overarching campaign tone into the native language of each business unit. Industrial infrastructure emphasized reliability and safety, airports focused on hospitality, and consumer platforms communicated quality. The core promise was unshakeable, but the expression matched each distinct stakeholder group.

Rather than launching isolated campaigns, we architected a cohesive narrative engine operating across paid, owned, and earned channels:

Paid Media
  • TVC across 80+ channels (20,000+ spots)
  • 10 regional languages, 400+ GRPs
  • Shark Tank & Indian Idol marquee spots
  • India vs England international cricket placement
Owned Channels
  • Group & BU-level social handles
  • 10+ vernacular digital channels
  • Ports of Progress narrative series
  • Parivartan community storytelling engine
Earned Media
  • Corporate PR & national media relations
  • Tier 2 & Tier 3 digital creator activations
  • Community narrative ownership & CSR equity
  • Sentiment-driven digital message adaptation

Executive leadership amplified these stories via LinkedIn and op-eds, while short-form reels and platform-native cuts extended organic reach—creating a single corporate reputation platform expressed through dozens of authentic voices.
