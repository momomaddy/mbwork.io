The Gateway Films

Seven airports, one welcome.

Each Adani-run airport gets its own "Gateway to Seamless Experience" film — the flagship entry point into the wider video library, built to carry the same tone from Mumbai to Mangaluru while still feeling local.

The films walk a passenger through the same emotional arc everywhere: flight reminders, check-in, duty-free and F&B on the go, cab booking, and a lounge close. The repetition is deliberate — it's what makes seven different airports read as one brand.
