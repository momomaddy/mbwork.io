Concept 01 · Gandhi Jayanti

Gandhi's Monkeys. CNG's Brief.

Adani Gas

Reinterpreting the iconic three wise monkeys as CNG, PNG, and EV advocates—each with their own sense—earned the brand a place in one of the most culturally loaded calendar moments of the year without a single forced product mention.

Insight & Strategy
Culturally Embedded Wordplay

By avoiding generic greeting cards and using Gandhi's monkeys to frame clean fuel benefits, the copy made the product the message.
