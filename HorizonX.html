Where Forever Young Begins

From medical necessity to life decision: How an integrated narrative architecture moved hear.com beyond awareness to engineer category-wide adoption for Horizon X.

Opportunity

hear.com users were actively visiting the platform and taking digital hearing tests. Awareness was not the barrier—adoption was. Prospective customers recognized their auditory difficulties but delayed consultation for years, sometimes decades.

Hearing aids carried the heavy societal baggage of age, disability, and clinical vulnerability. The behavioural resistance was rooted in self-identity: customers hesitated because accepting the device felt like conceding to a version of themselves they weren’t ready to embrace.

“The communication challenge wasn't awareness. It was self-identity.”

The opportunity was to transform hearing care from a clinical purchase into an empowering lifestyle upgrade—positioning Horizon X not as a corrective tool for decline, but as high-performance technology restoring full participation in life’s most vital moments.

Role

I directed the end-to-end communication architecture—from the initial high-intent ad impression through to post-adoption app onboarding—aligning product positioning across creative, performance marketing, and regional audiology teams:

ASP Strategy

Positioned the Horizon line as a premium exclusive on the Signia Xperience platform to lift Average Selling Price.

APAC Expansion

Led the multi-market narrative across India and 5 APAC regions to maintain 20% YoY growth entering 2021.

Feature Framing

Framed "Speech Focus" and "Relax Mode" features as active lifestyle enhancements rather than clinical aids.

Journey Design

Constructed a unified narrative bridge connecting paid social ads, conversion landing pages, and tele-consult calls.

CRO Optimization

Iterated funnels with performance teams using multivariate A/B testing and behavioral heatmaps.

Category Positioning

Elevated Horizon X alongside premium consumer hearables (e.g. AirPods) to remove stigma at the checkout.

Sequence

We moved away from symptom-led clinical messaging. The customer needed to feel seen and understood before any hardware was presented:

Reflect real-world conversational friction without clinical judgment or diagnostic jargon.
