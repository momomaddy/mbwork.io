One Nation Billion Celebrations

How a festive celeration campaign changed the way people experience Airports in Tier 2 & 3 cities...

Client Adani Airports
Campaign One Nation Billion Celebrations
Format Digital, airport, paid and on-ground
Awards 20+ Global Awards including Hermes, AVA Digital Awards
First-of-its-kind campaign driven entirely by digital brought celebrations under one umbrella across seven Indian airports.
$300M+ FY23 Non-Aero Revenue, Up by 45%
10M+ People reached through the campaign
5.3M Video views across the campaign
12x Surge in Organic Web Traffic
Portfolio Feature We weren't building campaigns. We were changing behaviour.

Covered in-depth by Moodie Davitt Report

The Brief

It was simple and came straight from the Chairman.

We intend to design futuristic airports, develop entertainment destinations (aerotropolis, airport village, hotels and malls, among others) and enhance domestic airline connectivity with new locations. We aim to create a Changi (Singapore’s premier airport) which has customers across age groups and where people visit not just to travel, but for leisure too.

And humanise physical infrastructure on and via digital channels for travellers and visitors.

The Challenge

1: Shift consumer perception of regional airports from cold transit hubs to premier lifestyle, cultural, and leisure destinations.

2: Drive high-margin non-aero revenues (retail, dining, duty-free, and leisurefootprint) by increasing dwell time, footfall, and average spend per passenger across Adani’s multi-airport network.

3: Build digital brand trust and organic affinity by creating emotionally resonant social & digital comms framework that removes traveler anxiety and friction expecially for first time travellers in Tier2 and 3 cities.

The Solution

Airports as Cultural Gateways & Destinations anchors.

We reframed the airport from a 'place you pass through' to 'the starting point of regional pride.' The opportunity was to think of one umbrella thought that links everything together, appeals to people of all ethnicities and convey a uniform message of celebration,

"ONBC" (#OneNationBillionCelebrations) was launched and conceptualised as the inaugural proof-of-concept. An omnichannel cultural movement during peak festival season to bring various festival celebrations of India under one umbrella and engage people by integrating live local heritage art on the ground with human-first storytelling on social media and turned routine airport touchpoints into emotional, shareable digital content

In India, we may have different names and traditions for different festivals, but the fervor and joy of celebrating them is equal and extraordinary.

The team at Adani Digital Labs, after extensive brainstorming, conceptualized the campaign in September 2021 and rolled it out in October with Navratri. The campaign idea being that in India, we may have different names and traditions for different festivals, but the fervor and joy of celebrating them is equal and extraordinary. That's what unites our great nation. India is thus one nation that has a billion celebrations going on.

The Execution

We needed to change passenger behavior:

Extend Dwell Time: Give travelers a reason to arrive hours earlier or linger after landing.

Attract Non-Travelers/City Dwellers: Position the airport village/aerotropolis ecosystem as a cultural anchor for the broader city.

Unify Regional Assets: Turn 7 distinct regional airports into a cohesive national retail and experiential network.

A multi-layered 360-degree, omnichannel rollout across paid/earned social media, SEO migration, custom digital assets, and live terminal experiences bridging on-ground experiential activations with digital distribution for 7 airports in India:.

Mumbai Ahmedabad Lucknow Mangaluru Guwahati Jaipur Thiruvananthapuram

Airport as a medium of communication:

Instead of treating the airport as passive physical infrastructure that required marketing, we weaponised the terminal space as our primary broadcast medium. By transforming regional airports into living, experiential content hubs—combining live cultural workshops, regional art installations, and touchpoint branding—we turned millions of travelers into active content creators, seamlessly bridging on-ground experiential engagement with multi-channel digital reach.

Immersive Passenger Engagement:

Rather than standard signage, the airports hosted live heritage art workshops—such as Warli Art, Mata ni Pachedi, Khavda Pottery, Kaavi Art, Mandala, and Floral Painting—giving travelers an interactive taste of local traditions.

Employee & Touchpoint Integration:

Staff wore themed #OneNationBillionCelebrations badges and masks, while terminal spaces featured thematic selfie booths, dangling installations, and festive coasters.

The Result

Revenue & Commercial Impact

The campaign directly fueled commercial outcomes in two primary ways:

Non-Aeronautical Retail Growth ("Shop & Win"): Following its initial branding success, the One Nation Billion Celebrations umbrella was expanded into targeted commercial activations across airports. By linking airport retail/F&B spending thresholds to assured gifts and raffle entries, the campaign directly boosted footfall conversions, average order values, and tenant revenue at departure and arrival terminals.

Adani One Digital Ecosystem Onboarding:By driving organic traffic and engagement to unified digital assets, the campaign significantly reduced user acquisition costs for Adani’s digital travel services (Adani One platform), generating recurring ecosystem value through flight/hotel bookings, duty-free pre-orders, and airport services.

F&B Revenue +52%, Duty-Free Revenue +32%, Income per passenger +30%

The #OneNationBillionCelebrations campaign continues today as recurrring annual festival season sales fest at the Adani Airports.

The Communications Strategy Takeaway

For Adani Airports, the debut of "One Nation Billion Celebrations" wasn't just a festive promotion; it was a corporate capability statement. By successfully executing a digitally-driven consumer campaign at this scale, the communications team proved to the market, aviation analysts, and institutional investors that they could seamlessly convert physical, industrial infrastructure footprints into highly monetizable, modern digital ecosystems. It shifts the airport’s identity from a mere utility (a place to catch a flight) to a lifestyle destination. By aligning with the "Make in India" and "Swadeshi" narratives—through the promotion of local craftsmen—it also strengthens the group's ESG (Environmental, Social, and Governance) and community engagement profile.
