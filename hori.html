Recent Work / Behind the Brief hear.com Horizon X
The Brief

From medical necessity to life decision.

Repositioning hear.com’s flagship hardware from a stigmatized clinical prosthetic into an aspirational consumer hearable across 6 APAC markets.

The Challenge
Users actively visited the site and completed hearing tests, but delayed action for years. Awareness wasn't broken—self-identity was. Accepting the device meant accepting aging.
The Strategic Shift
Borrow from premium consumer audio (Apple/AirPods). Horizon X doesn't correct a medical deficit; it restores social participation and everyday edge.
