01 · The Search

The Story is Always There

I believe every company has something interesting to say. Even the ones that seem impossibly dull.

The challenge is finding it. The good stuff rarely arrives on a silver platter. You have to dig, ask better questions, connect the dots, and sometimes look where nobody thought to look.

That's what I've spent the last decade doing.

03 · The Question

What's The Story?

The industries change. The question doesn't: What's the story?

Sometimes it's hidden in a customer insight. Sometimes in a strange number, an unexpected person, or an overlooked detail. Sometimes it takes a dozen conversations to find the thread.

And sometimes the story isn't there yet. That's when you build one.

I like working where business meets culture, and strategy meets storytelling. Where a complicated idea needs a simple human truth. Where a brand has something important to say but hasn't quite found the words.

Find the good stuff.

Connect the dots.

Make it matter.

Because the story is always there.
