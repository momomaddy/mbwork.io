About

Before I wrote my first line of copy or designed a corporate communications architecture, I sold directly to people on the front lines.

I grew up in Kashmir—a place where beauty and contradiction rarely arrive separately. Living and studying in Edinburgh taught me the complementary discipline: say less, mean more. Somewhere between those environments, I developed the primary skill that has anchored my career: paying close attention.

Over the last decade, I’ve helped startups, corporate groups, and public institutions navigate moments that matter—translating complex products and technical infrastructures into stories people understand and remember.
