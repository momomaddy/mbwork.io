Execution Scope

Relationship-first growth.

The Partner Privilege Program launch needed more than event management. It required operational readiness, leadership alignment, partner participation, and media coherence under one narrative about relationship-first growth.

Event Management: Event design, production flow, and vendor management.
Leadership Alignment: Cross-functional leadership alignment (Founders’ Office, business, operations, tech).
PR & Media: Media coordination, press interactions, and narrative reinforcement.
Partner Education: Partner education communication to support onboarding and product usage.
Tracking: Post-launch coverage tracking and leadership reporting.
