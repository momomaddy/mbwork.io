About

I grew up in Kashmir and later studied in Edinburgh. Between those places I learned two different ways of seeing the world: one taught complexity, the other clarity.

Before communications I sold books, worked in customer service, moderated focus groups and wrote for newsrooms. Those experiences still shape how I build brands today.

Over the last decade I've worked across infrastructure, hospitality, AI, healthcare, fintech and consumer technology, translating complex businesses into stories people understand and remember.

Outside work I'm building EH5 Stories and writing my first novel.
