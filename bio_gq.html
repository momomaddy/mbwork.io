Think like a strategist.
Move like an author.

The industries change. The question doesn't: What's the story? Sometimes it's hidden in a customer insight, sometimes in a strange number, an unexpected person, or an overlooked detail. And when the story isn't there yet—that's when you build one.

01

Understand the core

Corporate positioning, audience insights, board alignment, and diagnosing what you actually stand for before you publish.

02

Shape the narrative

C-suite speeches, brand films, and category reframing. Finding the simple human truth buried underneath complex corporate mechanics.

03

Run the engine

Multi-format rollout across social, digital handles, executive media, and paid distribution in 10+ vernacular languages.

04

Direct the momentum

Moving content through internal allies before it hits the street, ensuring stories create cultural traction instead of walking alone.

You do not need to shout to earn attention. You need to know what a human truth looks like, brief the people who carry it, and keep the whole narrative moving.

Three principles.
No corporate wallpaper.

Find the good stuff.

The good stuff rarely arrives on a silver platter. You have to dig past boilerplate press releases to uncover the contrarian detail that makes journalists and consumers lean in.

Connect the dots.

I like working where business meets culture, and strategy meets storytelling. Where an engineering milestone or supply chain network becomes a lived, personal reality.

Make it matter.

If you cannot whisper the truth, no media spend will make them believe the shout. True narrative endurance is built on credibility, delivery, and human recognition.
